Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how OpenClaw (“we,” “our,” or “the Service”) collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the OpenClaw iOS app and related backend services.

1. Data We Collect

Account and registration

  • Email address — when you register or sign in with email and password, or when you use Sign in with Apple (we may receive an Apple relay email or your email if you choose to share it).
  • Display name — the name you provide or that we derive (e.g. from your email or Apple identity).
  • Password — stored only in hashed form on our servers; we do not store your plain-text password.
  • Apple user identifier — when you use Sign in with Apple, we store the Apple-assigned user ID to link your account.

Authentication and tokens

  • Access and refresh tokens — we issue and store tokens so you can stay signed in and we can authenticate your requests to our API.
  • OAuth tokens — when you connect third-party services (e.g. Notion or other integrations) through the app, we store the access and refresh tokens provided by those services so the AI agents can act on your behalf. These are stored on our servers and associated with your account and the relevant integration.

Usage data

  • Usage metrics — we record aggregated usage per account per day, such as number of tasks, tokens used, and voice usage (e.g. seconds), to enforce plan limits and operate the Service.

AI and conversation content

  • Messages and attachments — when you send messages or attachments in the app (e.g. in chat or task flows), this content is sent to our backend and processed by our systems and AI providers to generate responses. We use this data to provide the Service and may retain it as needed for operation, security, and legal compliance.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data above to:

  • Create and manage your account and authenticate you.
  • Provide the AI assistant, task execution, and connected integrations.
  • Enforce subscription and usage limits (e.g. free vs. Pro).
  • Operate, secure, and improve the Service and to comply with legal obligations.

3. Storage and Security

Your data is stored on our servers (and, where applicable, with service providers we use). We use industry-standard measures to protect data in transit and at rest. Passwords are hashed; we do not store plain-text passwords.

4. Third Parties

We may share or process data with:

  • Apple — for Sign in with Apple and in-app purchases; Apple’s privacy policy applies to those interactions.
  • AI / LLM providers — conversation content may be sent to third-party AI services we use to generate responses.
  • OAuth providers — when you connect integrations (e.g. Notion), we exchange and store tokens per their OAuth flows.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties for their marketing.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us (see below). You can also delete your account through the app or by requesting account deletion from us.

6. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such data, please contact us so we can delete it.

7. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the revised policy at this page and update the “Last updated” date. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance. We may notify you of material changes in the app or by email where appropriate.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions or to exercise your rights, please open an issue or contact the maintainers via the OpenClaw iOS repository on GitHub.
